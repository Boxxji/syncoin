## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence inference demand has grown exponentially, outpacing electrical grid infrastructure. Centralized hyperscaler data centers consume gigawatts of fossil energy and billions of liters of potable cooling water while waiting up to 6 years for grid connections.

**SynCoin** introduces the **Residential Micro-Neocloud**: an open-source, hardware-agnostic P2P protocol that eliminates data center grid bottlenecks by executing distributed AI inference directly where clean energy is generated—on rooftop solar panels, home battery storage systems (BESS), and charging consumer devices.

Through an automated **100% direct value-sharing model**, SynCoin transforms excess clean residential power into real-time token revenue for individual households without corporate middlemen.

---

## 1. The Energy Bottleneck & The Grid Bypass

Traditional cloud hyperscalers transport electricity over hundreds of kilometers to concentrated computing facilities. SynCoin inverts this equation:
- **Move compute to energy**: The inference workload travels over the Internet directly to the rooftop where the sun is currently shining.
- **Water-Free Cooling**: Residential nodes use passive and ambient air cooling, eliminating data center water waste.
- **Sun-Follower P2P Queue**: Requests are dynamically dispatched across global timezones to follow daylight solar curves.

---

## 2. Universal 4-Tier Hardware Topology

1. **Smartphones & Tablets (Edge Layer)**: Embedded WebAssembly runtime (Wasm3) on Apple Neural Engine and Android NPUs.
2. **Desktop GPUs & Workstations (Inference Layer)**: Nvidia CUDA, Apple Metal, AMD ROCm running open-weight SLMs.
3. **Residential Micro-Neocloud (Energy Layer)**: Home batteries and solar inverters managed by `syncoin_energy_daemon.py`.
4. **Decentralized Marketplace (Gateway Layer)**: OpenAI-compatible API (`syncoin_gateway.py`) enabling universal access.
