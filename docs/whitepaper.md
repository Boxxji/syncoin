## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence inference has surpassed compute hardware availability by 20x, creating a global energy bottleneck. Data centers consume gigawatts of grid power and billions of liters of potable water while waiting up to 6 years for grid connections.

**SynCoin** introduces the **Residential Micro-Neocloud**: an open-source, hardware-agnostic protocol that bypasses the electrical grid entirely by executing distributed AI inference directly at the source of clean energy—home solar panels, battery energy storage systems (BESS), and charging smartphones.

Through an automated 90/10 remuneration model, SynCoin transforms fatal clean energy into real-time token revenue for everyday households while funding verified planetary reforestation.

---

## 1. The Energy Bottleneck & The Grid Bypass

Traditional cloud hyperscalers concentrate gigawatts of compute in centralized facilities. SynCoin reverses this paradigm:
- **Move compute to energy**: The compute job travels to the residential rooftop where the sun is currently shining.
- **Water-Free Cooling**: Residential nodes use passive or ambient air cooling, eliminating data center water waste.
- **Sun-Follower Priority Queue**: Requests are dynamically dispatched across timezones to follow daylight curves globally.

## 2. Universal 4-Tier Hardware Topology

1. **Smartphones & Tablets (Edge Layer)**: Embedded WebAssembly runtime (Wasm3) on Apple Neural Engine and Android NPUs.
2. **Desktop GPUs & Workstations (Inference Layer)**: Nvidia CUDA, Apple Metal, AMD ROCm running open-weight SLMs.
3. **Residential Micro-Neocloud (Energy Layer)**: Home batteries and solar inverters managed by `syncoin_energy_daemon.py`.
4. **Decentralized Marketplace (Gateway Layer)**: OpenAI-compatible API (`syncoin_gateway.py`) enabling commercial access.
